SlopFilter privacy

There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, or servers. Filtering runs locally in this browser.

Filtering decisions, statistics, and downvote history stay in this browser’s local storage.

Pinned model files are downloaded from huggingface.co and its model CDN. When a post has an inline X image, SlopFilter may fetch that thumbnail from pbs.twimg.com to read C2PA content credentials; it never requests full-resolution media. Image checks fail open when the thumbnail is unavailable, and fetched bytes are not stored.

Verify it yourself

  1. Open DevTools for the X tab.
  2. Open the Network tab and clear the request list.
  3. After the models are cached, browse the timeline and confirm SlopFilter only requests model files and visible X thumbnails.
