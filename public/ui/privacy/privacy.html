SlopFilter privacy

What SlopFilter guarantees

  • Runs with no accounts, analytics, telemetry, or servers.
  • Filtering runs locally in this browser.
  • Filtering decisions, statistics, and downvote history stay in this browser’s local storage.
  • Pinned model files are downloaded from huggingface.co and its model CDN.
  • Inline X images may be fetched as thumbnails from pbs.twimg.com to read C2PA content credentials. Full-resolution media is never requested.
  • Image checks fail open when a thumbnail is unavailable, and fetched bytes are not stored.

Verify it yourself

  1. Open DevTools for the X tab.
  2. Open the Network tab and clear the request list.
  3. After the models are cached, browse the timeline and confirm SlopFilter only requests model files and visible X thumbnails.
